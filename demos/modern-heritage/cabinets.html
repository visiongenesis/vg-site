The Smart Alternative

Love your kitchen again. Keep your cabinets.

If your cabinets are structurally sound but tired to look at, refinishing beats replacement three ways: it costs a fraction as much, it takes days instead of weeks, and nothing ends up in a landfill. We clean, strip the old finish, sand, and apply your new stain or paint — oak, cherry, walnut, maple tones, or a fresh painted color.

We refinish kitchen cabinets, bathroom vanities, and built-ins, and we handle full cabinet restoration for older pieces worth saving. Most kitchens stay usable through the process.

Typical Knoxville-area range: a full kitchen refinish runs $3,500–$8,500 depending on size, doors, and finish — versus $15,000–$30,000+ for replacement. A typical kitchen takes 4–6 working days.

Questions

Good questions we hear a lot

Is refinishing really more cost-effective than replacement?

Yes — typically a third to a half of replacement cost, because your existing boxes and doors are reused. It's also faster and far less disruptive: days, not weeks.

Can you change my cabinet color completely?

Yes. Stained-to-painted, painted-to-stained, dark-to-light — after stripping and prep we can take the cabinets essentially anywhere you want them.

Can any cabinet be refinished?

Most solid-wood and wood-veneer cabinets refinish beautifully. Some laminate or damaged boxes are better candidates for repainting or replacement — we'll tell you honestly at the estimate.

Will I be able to use my kitchen during the work?

Mostly, yes. We stage the work, use dust collection, and keep the space livable. There will be short windows when doors and drawers are curing.

How long does it take?

A typical kitchen runs 4–6 working days; a bathroom vanity 1–2 days. We confirm the schedule in your written quote.
