Built Outside, Finished Like Inside

An outdoor room, not just a platform.

A good deck extends your home. We design yours around how you'll actually use it — morning coffee, big-family cookouts, a quiet lake view — then build it structurally right with quality materials, and finish it with the same care we give interior trim: paint, stain, and details that last.

We design and build new decks, screened porches, and dock and outdoor structures, and we restore existing ones with repair, paint, and stain work.

Typical Knoxville-area range: custom decks run $9,000–$28,000+ depending on size, height, and materials; most builds take 2–4 weeks. Restaining an existing deck is far less — ask for a quote.

Questions

Good questions we hear a lot

Do you design the deck or just build it?

Both. We work with you on a design that fits your home, your yard, and your budget, then our master carpenter builds it.

What about permits?

Deck projects in the Knoxville area often need a permit, and requirements vary by jurisdiction. We'll flag what your project needs and point you to the right office during the estimate.

Paint or stain?

Depends on the wood, the sun exposure, and the look you want — we do both and will recommend honestly for your spot.

How long does a deck take?

Most builds run 2–4 weeks from first board to final coat, weather permitting. Restains and repairs are usually a few days.

What does a custom deck cost?

Typically $9,000–$28,000+ in this area depending on size, height, and materials. You'll get an exact written price after a free on-site visit.
