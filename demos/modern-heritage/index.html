Why Modern Heritage

Small on purpose. Skilled by design.

Modern Heritage began in 2017 (as Southern Painting and Trim) to take on the hardest task in the carpentry trade: trim and finishes proportioned to complement your space — whether that's Georgian strength, Greek Revival simplicity, Victorian ornament, or Arts & Crafts handiwork.

  • Master carpenter on staff — not a fly-by-night crew
  • State-of-the-art dust collection; we leave it cleaner than we found it
  • High-touch communication — you always know where your project stands
  • We work around your family and your work-from-home day
