Our Story

From Southern Painting and Trim to Modern Heritage.

Modern Heritage Trim and Finishing got its start as Southern Painting and Trim back in 2017. After more than ten years in the custom painting and trim business, Managing Partner Mark Dix saw the need for a small, focused company to take on the most difficult task in the carpentry trade: well-proportioned trim and finishes that complete the message our clients want their space to send.

Whether that message is the masculine strength of historic Georgian and Federal styles, the clean lines of Greek Revival, the ornament of a Victorian home, or the honest handiwork of the Arts & Crafts period — we're dedicated to bringing it to life.

When you engage our team, you're not hiring a fly-by-night crew with a saw and a hammer. We are fully licensed, bonded, and insured, with a list of references you can call. We believe in high-level communication — you always know where your project stands — and we bring concierge-level care to your build: design consultation, state-of-the-art dust collection, and scheduling that works around your work-from-home life.
