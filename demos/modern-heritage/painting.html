The Finish Coat

Paint is the fastest transformation there is — when it's done right.

A new paint job renews a space like nothing else, and it's where corners get cut most often. We come from the painting trade — Modern Heritage's founders ran a painting business before this one — so prep is where we spend our time: clean surfaces, tight lines, immaculate results.

Interior: walls, ceilings, trim, doors, cabinets, floors, and epoxy floor coatings. Exterior: siding, full house repaints, decks and fences (paint or stain), and garage doors. We help you choose colors that fit your home's light and era.

Typical Knoxville-area ranges: interior rooms $450–$1,200 each depending on size and ceiling height; whole-house exteriors quoted after a free walk-around. A standard room repaint takes 2–3 days including cure time.

Questions

Good questions we hear a lot

Do you do both interior and exterior?

Yes — and surfaces most painters avoid: cabinets, floors, epoxy coatings, decks, fences, and garage doors.

How do I pick colors?

We offer a design consultation and can point you to combinations that suit your home's style and light. Bring your ideas; we'll bring samples.

How long does a room take?

A standard room is typically 2–3 days including prep and cure. Whole-house projects get a written schedule with the quote.

Do you protect my home while you work?

Everything gets masked and covered, we run dust collection on sanding work, and we tidy the site daily. Work-from-home household? We plan around your calls.

What does interior painting cost?

Typically $450–$1,200 per room in the Knoxville area, depending on size, ceilings, and trim work. Exact price comes in a free written estimate.
