The Craft

Trim is the difference you can't unsee.

Moulding has dressed great buildings for thousands of years. In your home it does the same quiet work: crown eases the line between wall and ceiling, casings frame every door and window, and baseboards ground the room. Custom moulding takes it further — profiles chosen for your ceiling height, your light, your home's period.

We build and install crown, baseboard, window and door casings, ceiling treatments (coffered, beamed, tray), wainscoting and accent walls, decorative moulding, and full custom built-ins. If your home has existing trim from the 1920s or the 1990s, we can match the profile so new work disappears into old.

Typical Knoxville-area ranges: crown moulding $600–$2,500 per room · wainscoting or a paneled accent wall $1,200–$4,500 · coffered ceiling $2,800–$8,500 · custom built-ins $3,000–$12,000. Every quote is written for your actual space.

Questions

Good questions we hear a lot

How does the custom moulding process work?

We start with a design consultation: we look at your rooms, ceiling heights, and the style of your home, then recommend profiles and proportions. You approve a written quote, we build and install, and we finish with paint or stain so it looks like it was always there.

Can you match the existing moulding in my home?

Almost always, yes. We can replicate most existing profiles — including older ones that aren't sold in stores — so additions and repairs blend seamlessly with the original trim.

Do you install, or just build?

Both. We manufacture or source the moulding and install and finish it. One crew, one standard, start to finish.

What rooms benefit most from moulding?

Living and dining rooms, entries, and primary bedrooms see the biggest transformation. Coffered or tray ceilings make a great room feel architect-designed; wainscoting turns a hallway or dining room from plain to finished.

What does it cost?

As a rule of thumb in the Knoxville area: crown runs $600–$2,500 per room depending on size and profile, paneled walls $1,200–$4,500, coffered ceilings $2,800–$8,500, and custom built-ins $3,000–$12,000. We'll give you an exact written price after a free on-site visit.
