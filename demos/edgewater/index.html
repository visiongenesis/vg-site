Honest notes — read before trusting a row. This is a proof-of-concept on a real snapshot pulled from public, view-only portals. In production the feed re-checks each portal on a schedule and alerts on new or changed postings. Forecast items are opportunities an operator expects to issue (the date is the operator's expected issue, not a live deadline); Open items are live solicitations with a stated due date. Contract-value bands tagged "Est." are our estimate from category and comparable work — not a figure the operator published; bands tagged "Published" are copied from the operator's own page. Scope summaries, document lists, and named buyers shown in the detail panel are resolved live from each operator's package in production — the preview fills them representatively. Verify every row against the linked source before acting.

Not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the U.S. Department of Energy or any site contractor. Sources are each operator's own public procurement portal. Built as a capability demonstration for internal review.
