Competitive assessment

The opening is the family operating system.

The market already has brackets, team apps, camp directories, and athlete goal tools. The defensible opportunity is the connective tissue: one parent-owned plan spanning every wrestler, team, event source, registration state, result, and development goal.

What the market says

Not empty. Still open.

No reviewed product visibly combines the entire journey. But nearly every individual feature already has a capable owner, so parity features cannot carry the pitch.

01

Family profiles are table stakes

Bleachers, TeamSnap, and GameChanger already unify children, teams, schedules, and family access. Multi-wrestler planning must be the starting point, not the moat.

02

Discovery is already aggregating

iWrestle, MapWrestling, WrestlingPartner, and Flo make events easier to find. Win on verified freshness, camps, eligibility, family fit, and planning state.

03

Bracketing is a partner layer

FloArena, Trackwrestling, USA Bracketing, Snapdown, and Smoothcomp own event-day truth. Link, import, and normalize before trying to replace them.

How research changed the product

Six deliberate design calls.

1

Home is a household command center

“What is next, what needs action, and where are the conflicts?” outranks a generic stats dashboard.

2

Every listing carries trust metadata

Official source, registration state, and last-checked date are visible product features—not fine print.

3

Planning has a real state machine

Save → Considering → Registered → Completed connects discovery to calendars and later to results.

4

Goals are evidence-backed

Attendance, completed events, and results move progress. Parents get a next best action, not decorative bars.

5

Community begins private

Parent-owned access, verified coaches, team boards, and event Q&A precede any open youth social layer.

6

Recruiting stays narrow

The prototype exports a private season summary. Coach discovery and outreach wait for client requirements and safeguards.

Monetization hypothesis

Free coverage. Paid leverage.

Broad discovery only works if families and operators both want complete coverage.

Families

Free core + one family plan

Free profiles, search, saves, calendar, and source links. Premium for conflict detection, advanced reminders, goal automation, history, analytics, and richer sharing—priced per family, never per child.

Operators

Free claim + paid visibility

Free basic listing and verification. Paid enhanced branding, featured placement, lead/conversion insight, waitlists, and later transaction or referral revenue.

Teams

Coordination layer later

Bulk family access, schedule imports, curated opportunity lists, and coach-created goal templates—without replacing a team’s existing roster or bracket system.
