Staying
Resident means all‑in

Room at the Spooky Nook hotel and every meal are included in the price shown. Standard rooms are four wrestlers, grouped by camp, grade and weight. A private bed is available as a bed buy‑out at $50 a night.

Commuter means daily

Your wrestler trains every session and eats the meals served on camp days, then goes home at night. It is the lower price because there is no hotel room in it.

Bringing more than one

Teams and groups of four or more wrestlers take $25 to $75 off each, and the coach attends free. Email camps@kenchertow.com and Ken, Beth and the team will set it up.

04The Week

What a week actually
looks like.

Three sessions a day, two to three hours each. Technique, drilling, live wrestling and review — the same structure Ken has run for more than three decades.

Every day

Three sessions

Two to three hours on the mat per session, three times a day. World-class technical instruction, intensive drilling and daily live wrestling.

The method

Learn, drill, live, review

Technique broken down by position, high-rep drilling to build the movement, live wrestling to test it, then systematic review sessions so it survives the drive home.

Off the mat

Supervised, all week

A designated counselor per group, morning wake-ups, bed checks, a 10pm curfew and a no-departure policy. Ratios are 10 to 1 for teens and 6 to 1 for youth.

On site

Nurse and meals

A camp nurse is on site. Medication is self-administered when noted on the application, otherwise nurse-managed. Every wrestler’s meals are included.

Check-in

1:00 to 3:00pm on the first day. Ohio: Spooky Nook, 601 N B St., Hamilton. Pennsylvania: Spooky Nook, 75 Champ Blvd., Manheim. First session is that afternoon — dinner is the first meal we serve.

Getting there

Airport shuttle is $50 one way, $100 round trip, per person — Cincinnati for Ohio, Harrisburg for Pennsylvania. Send the itinerary to beth@kenchertow.com.

Roommates

Put the request on the application, or email Beth before June 1 and she will handle it. Rooms are assigned by camp type, grade and weight.
