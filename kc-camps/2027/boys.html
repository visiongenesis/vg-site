The Gold Medal Training Camp System

Pick your camp.

Longer camps drive bigger results. The more time a wrestler spends in structured training, the more real growth you see. From a 4-day camp to the full SuperGold intensive, every option is built to get the most out of your wrestler and move them toward their goals.

A day at camp

Train. Eat. Rest. Repeat.

Resident campers get four-star hotel accommodations, three meals a day and supervision. Commuters get the training and meals. Camp is highly supervised, with an 8:1 staff-to-camper ratio.

  • 7 a.m. in the weight roomStructured weight training is a standard opt-in part of camp. Camp counselors lead 7 a.m. workouts throughout the week in the weight rooms at Spooky Nook.
  • A hot breakfastThree a day, starting with a hot breakfast buffet. Campers, counselors and coaches eat together.
  • Sharper techniqueOlympians and NCAA champions teach in small groups. Then your wrestler drills each move until the details are right, from the setup to the finish.
  • Live wrestlingLive wrestling every day against partners who push back. By the end of camp, a close third period is something they've felt plenty of times.
  • Evening speakersEvening guest speakers and sports-science seminars teach recovery and nutrition, so your wrestler knows how to keep improving long after camp.
  • Lights out at 10 p.m.Assigned counselors take roll at every session and run bed checks every night. Lights out at 10 p.m.
