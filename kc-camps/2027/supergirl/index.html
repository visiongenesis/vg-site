★ For parents

For the grown-ups.

Ken and Beth Chertow run the camps as a family. Beth is the camp administrator and runs camp day to day.

Where she stays

Resident campers get four-star hotel accommodations in two-queen rooms at the Warehouse Hotel. In Pennsylvania it sits on the Spooky Nook campus. In Ohio it is across the street from the sports complex.

Meals

Three meals a day for resident campers. Commuters get training plus meals.

Supervision

Camp is highly supervised, with an 8:1 staff-to-camper ratio. Counselors take roll at every session and run bed checks every night.

Past staff

Past SuperGirl staff include Kylie Welker, Briana Kellin, Marina Doi and Sage Mortimer.

Where and when

Spooky Nook Sports, Manheim, Pennsylvania (Lancaster County): July 11–22, 2027. Spooky Nook Champion Mill, Hamilton, Ohio: June 19–24, 2027.

Cost

SuperGirl is Ken's 12-day camp. See 2027 pricing.

Home base: Spooky Nook Sports

Is SuperGirl girls-only?

For training, yes. Girls drill and wrestle live in girls-only groups. They share mat space with the boys' camps, and our SuperGirls get the same world-class guest clinicians as the boys. That covers the 12-day SuperGirl camp, the 6-day Gold Medal Training Camp and the 4-day Girls Technique camp.

Can younger girls come?

Yes. Future Champions is a 4-day parent-child camp for grades 1–3, offered in both Pennsylvania and Ohio. It's a mixed camp, boys and girls together.

Where and when is camp in 2027?

Pennsylvania: July 11–22, 2027, at Spooky Nook Sports in Manheim. Ohio: June 19–24, 2027, at Spooky Nook Champion Mill in Hamilton. The 2027 staff will be announced soon.
