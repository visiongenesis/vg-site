Kids Training Camp and Future Champions: bringing more than one child

A second child shares the parent's room and registers at the commuter price.

  • Kids Training Camp: when you register the second child, choose the additional child option (same room): $950 for the 6-⁠day camp, $850 for the 5-⁠day camp.
  • Future Champions: register the second child as a commuter: $650.

A third child: also the commuter price, plus $100 off with code THIRDCHILD.
