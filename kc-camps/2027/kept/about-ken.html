Ken Chertow – A Legacy of Excellence in Wrestling

Ken Chertow is a U.S. Olympian, 3x NCAA All-American, and renowned coach. Ken has dedicated his life to shaping champion wrestlers. From competing on the world’s biggest stages to mentoring thousands of wrestlers, his Gold Medal Training Camps continue to inspire the next generation of athletes.

Timeline of Accomplishments

Coach Ken Chertow has excelled at every level of wrestling, both as a competitor and as a coach. His journey began at age 11 and took him all the way to the Olympics. Today he is the head coach of the longest-running wrestling camp system in the nation. 

1980s

Wrestling Beginnings

Early Wrestling Success

In the 5th grade Ken's mom introduced him wrestling. After 5 years of consistent training and summer camps, Ken became a 2x undefeated state champion. He won 2 Outstanding Wrestler Awards in high school, culminating in Junior National titles in both freestyle and Greco. Ken won 2 Junior World Championships and the foundation was set for pursuit of Olympic Gold. 

1985–1989

NCAA & Olympics

College & Olympic Career

At Penn State University, Ken took his skills to the next level, becoming a three-time NCAA All-American, Pan American Champion, and a U.S. Olympian. Off the mat, he excelled academically, earning three Academic All-American honors and graduating with a 3.6 GPA.

1990s

Coaching Begins

Building Champions

Ken’s coaching career began while he was still competing, running summer camps that would later evolve into the Gold Medal Training Camp System. His coaching tenure at Ohio State and Penn State helped shape powerhouse programs, with Ohio State achieving its highest NCAA finishes at the time (4th and 5th place) and Penn State securing a National Dual Meet Championship and an NCAA runner-up finish—their best in nearly 50 years.

2000s

Gold Medal Camps

Training Future Stars

Now, Ken’s focus is entirely on mentoring and developing the next generation of champions. His Gold Medal Training Camps are recognized as some of the most successful wrestling camps in the nation, helping athletes refine their technique, build mental toughness, and reach their full potential. Thousands of his wrestlers have gone on to win State and National Championships. 10+ have made it to the Olympics, including Zain Retherford, a key coach at camp. 

2010s

Expanding Reach

Clinician & Broadcaster

Ken shares his expertise as a broadcast analyst for the Pac-12 Network and Big Ten Network (BTN), leads clinics across the country, and has authored ‘Wrestling: A Commitment to Excellence,’ a motivational guide to success in wrestling and life.

Present

Leading Today

Coaching & Mentorship

Ken continues to train and mentor wrestlers through his Gold Medal Training Camps and clinics nationwide.
