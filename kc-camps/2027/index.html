The Gold Medal Training Camp System

Pick your camp.

Longer camps drive bigger results. The more time a wrestler spends in structured training, the more real growth you see. Ken's Gold Medal Training Camp System stacks up hours of focused drilling, plenty of live wrestling and enough repetition that new skills hold up in a real match. From a 4-day camp to the full SuperGold intensive, every option is built to get the most out of your wrestler and move them toward their goals. All camps offer resident and commuter options.

Built for the season ahead

What your wrestler will gain

  • Sharper techniqueOlympians and NCAA champions teach in small groups. Then your wrestler drills each move until the details are right, from the setup to the finish.
  • Calm in close matchesLive wrestling every day against partners who push back. By the end of camp, a close third period is something they've felt plenty of times.
  • Habits that come homeA hot breakfast, full days on the mat, lights out at 10 p.m. Day after day, that routine turns into habits your wrestler keeps all season.
  • A smarter wrestlerReview sessions cover the why behind each move, like when to attack and what to hit when the first shot gets stopped. Your wrestler learns to think a match through.
  • An athlete for the long runEvening guest speakers and sports-science seminars teach recovery and nutrition, so your wrestler knows how to keep improving long after camp.
  • A competitor's mindsetKen's staff includes Olympians and college head coaches who have been where your wrestler wants to go. Campers learn how champions set goals and how they bounce back from a loss.

Why 12 days makes a difference

The 12-day camps run at Spooky Nook Sports in Pennsylvania, July 11–22, 2027. Here's what two full weeks gives your wrestler.

  • Rep after repTwelve days of teaching, drilling and live wrestling. Coaches return to the same positions again and again, and your wrestler drills each one until it's automatic.
  • Moves that stay learnedWhat gets taught in week one gets reviewed in week two. When the season starts, it's still there.
  • Confidence you can seeBy the second week, a move that felt awkward early on starts scoring in live wrestling. Your wrestler sees it work and starts to trust it.
  • A head start on winterSummer wrestling makes winter champions. Twelve days of serious training sends your wrestler into the season with new skills already drilled in.

Parent FAQ

Questions parents ask.

Still unsure? Email the camp office at camps@kenchertow.com.

How do I pick the right camp?

Start with grade and experience. Future Champions is for grades 1–3. Newer wrestlers do well at the 4-day Technique Camp, and the 6-day Gold Medal Training Camp is a full week for developing wrestlers.

SuperGold, SuperGirl and SuperKid offer 12 days for committed wrestlers, the most complete wrestling development experience we offer. They're built for wrestlers who want more than a few good workouts. Over two weeks, structured training and daily discipline in a serious learning environment help them grow as wrestlers, as competitors and as people. The camp finder above gives you a starting point.

Is camp a good fit for a newer wrestler?

Yes. Beginners are welcome, and we encourage them to come. The 4-day Technique Camp is built for newer wrestlers, and Future Champions welcomes first-timers in grades 1–3.

Where is camp?

At Spooky Nook Sports. In Pennsylvania, that is Spooky Nook Sports in Manheim, Lancaster County (July 11–22, 2027). In Ohio, it is Spooky Nook Champion Mill in Hamilton (June 19–24, 2027). Resident campers stay in two-queen rooms at the Warehouse Hotel on each campus.

What does resident camp include?

Four-star hotel accommodations in a two-queen room at the Warehouse Hotel for the length of camp, three meals a day and a licensed RN on hand 24/7.

Camp is highly supervised. There is an 8:1 staff-to-camper ratio, and counselors take roll at every session and run bed checks every night.

Can my wrestler commute instead?

Yes. Commuters get the full training schedule and meals, then go home each night. There is a commuter discount.

Who's in charge day to day?

Ken runs the training every day, all day, with his committed staff of hand-picked championship coaches and college wrestlers. His wife, Beth, the camp administrator, runs everything off the mat. Beth taught middle school for 22 years and brings that same care, attention and structure to every camper's experience. This is the Chertow family.

Who does my wrestler train with?

We ask wrestlers to choose partners close to their own age and weight. Staff keep watch over the pairs so training stays safe and productive.

Is there internet? What about security?

High-speed internet is available. Security cameras cover most common areas. There are no cameras in guest rooms.

Is there a camp for girls?

Yes. Ken offers the premier girls' summer wrestling experience in the nation. Visit the SuperGirl site.

When is camp in 2027?

Pennsylvania: July 11–22, 2027. Ohio: June 19–24, 2027. The 2027 coaching lineup will be announced soon.

What about July 4th?

When a camp week includes July 4th, campers join our July 4th celebration at the facility. The 2027 camps fall outside the holiday: Ohio runs June 19–24, 2027 and Pennsylvania runs July 11–22, 2027.

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

We offer optional camp insurance through Purchase Protection LLC. With it, you can cancel up to 48 hours after the camp start date, subject to the provider's terms.
