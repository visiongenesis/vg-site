Parent FAQ

Questions parents ask.

Still unsure? Email the camp office at camps@kenchertow.com.

How do I pick the right camp?

Start with grade and experience. Newer wrestlers do well at the 4-day Technique Camp. The 6-day Gold Medal Training Camp is a full week for developing wrestlers. SuperGold's 12 days are for committed teens. The camp finder above gives you a starting point.

Where is camp?

At Spooky Nook Sports. In Pennsylvania, that is Spooky Nook Sports in Manheim, Lancaster County (July 11–22, 2027). In Ohio, it is Spooky Nook Sports Champion Mill in Hamilton (June 19–24, 2027). Resident campers stay in two-queen rooms at the Warehouse Hotel on each campus.

What does resident camp include?

A two-queen room at the Warehouse Hotel for the length of camp, three meals a day, assigned bed-check and roll-call counselors, and a camp nurse.

Can my wrestler commute instead?

Yes. Commuters get the full training schedule and meals, then go home each night.

Who is in charge day to day?

Ken Chertow runs the training. Beth Chertow, the camp administrator, runs everything off the mat. It is a family-run camp.

Is there a camp for girls?

Yes. SuperGirl is a 12-day girls camp, and Girls Technique is a 4-day camp. Visit the SuperGirl site.

When is camp in 2027?

Pennsylvania: July 11–22, 2027. Ohio: June 19–24, 2027. The 2027 coaching lineup is announced soon. The staff on this page is the 2026 staff, and the photos are from past camps.
