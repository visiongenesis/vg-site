The model

One pod. The work of three.

The constraint in AI implementation isn’t compute — it’s judgment and trust. The network model packages both.

Operators

Veteran business operators who generate the problems worth solving, set strategy, hold the truth, and mentor. They bring the judgment — and the door.

Builders

AI-native technologists who learn the Operator’s domain and ship working software at speed. Builders hold real upside in what they ship — equity follows delivery, not tenure.

The agent team

An ambient AI coordination layer under every pod — morning briefs, client ledgers, overnight build shifts. The firm runs on the same discipline it installs: we are our own first case study.
