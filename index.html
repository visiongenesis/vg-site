A closer look · Voice-to-Quote

A week of paperwork.
Gone before the drive home.

He runs a landscape business. Good crews, full calendar, great name. And every new job started the same way: walk the property, scribble notes, drive back, then burn nights and weekends turning those notes into estimates. Five to seven days from walkthrough to quote — while the customer called two competitors and waited on nobody.

Now he walks the property and talks. He describes what he sees, speaks the measurements, thinks out loud about materials. Norris listens, prices it against his own catalog, and drafts the estimate. It's ready before he's back at the office — and the customer who used to wait a week gets a quote while they're still warm and ready to act.
