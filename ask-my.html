The questions you're already asking

Straight answers.

How do you keep it from guessing about my business?

AskMy answers from your approved published content and runs a grounding check before sending. If the source does not support an answer, it says so and hands the question to a person. You review the private preview before launch.

Why not just use ChatGPT?

ChatGPT answers from the whole internet. AskMy answers from your handbook, your hours, your staff — and only that. It's your business talking, not the internet guessing.

I'm not technical. Can I really do this?

Yes. You paste your website, we build it, you click Publish. There's nothing to install and nothing to code. If you can fill out a checkout page, you can run this.

What if it gets something wrong?

You fix it in plain English from your dashboard and it updates in about a minute. No tickets, no waiting on us.

What about an emergency message?

Crisis messages route to real hotlines and your own care contact by code — not by hoping the AI does the right thing.

Is my data safe? Shared with other customers?

Tenant data is isolated. The public assistant uses approved published content. Private sources are not included unless you deliberately connect them for a scoped job.

What does it cost to get going?

One plan: $99 per month, no setup fee. The private preview is free and requires no card; billing starts only if you approve launch.
