For families

Your wrestler earned a 50% scholarship. Here's how to claim it.

A Union County coach nominated your wrestler for The Gold Medal Scholar-Athlete Award — a 50% scholarship from Ken Chertow's Gold Medal Training Camps toward Ken's six-day Olympic camp. It's an honor, and it's earned: a champion on the mat and in the classroom. To claim it, your family funds the other half — $475.

What the week is

Six days of Olympic-level coaching, elite partners, and room & board at Spooky Nook · Cincinnati, Ohio — July 18–23.

Your wrestler becomes a Gold Medal Scholar — and wears the shirt. A title they earned, on their chest.

The ride is on us

Vision Genesis Partners provides transportation for the whole team — to Ohio and back.

You don't drive to Ohio. Vision Genesis Partners sponsors the transportation, to camp and home again. And it isn't just your scholar-athlete — any teammate can come for $100 off tuition (code 2026TN100) and ride free, both ways. It's a Knoxville-wide program, and we want to send as many Knoxville wrestlers as possible.
