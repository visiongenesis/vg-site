Your wrestler earned a 50% scholarship. Here's how to claim it.

A Fulton coach nominated your wrestler for The Gold Medal Scholar-Athlete Award — a 50% scholarship from Ken Chertow's Gold Medal Training Camps toward Ken's six-day Olympic camp. It's an honor, and it's earned: a champion on the mat and in the classroom. To claim it, the family or club funds the other half — $475.

What the week is

Six days of Olympic-level coaching, elite practice partners, and training with room & board at Spooky Nook · Hamilton, Ohio — July 18–23. The kind of week that changes how a kid sees what's possible. And because it's in Ohio now, not Pennsylvania, it's more accessible to East Tennessee than it's ever been.

Your wrestler becomes a Gold Medal Scholar — and wears the shirt. A title they earned, on their chest.
The ride is on us

Vision Genesis drives the whole team — both ways.

You don't drive to Ohio. Vision Genesis sponsors the transportation, to camp and home again. And it isn't just your scholar-athlete: any teammate can come for $100 off tuition (code KNOX100) and ride free, both ways, right alongside your award winners. This is a Knoxville-wide program — the goal is to fill a charter bus with the hardest workers from across town, headed to Ohio together.

Optional — a local business can get behind it

Want a hometown business to sponsor your wrestler?

Plenty of families and clubs bring a local business in on the $475 — and we make the ask easy. A business that chips in $225 or more gets featured in Fulton's press & marketing, plus a free AI Business Bottleneck Unlock from Vision Genesis — a $1,500 value for them.
