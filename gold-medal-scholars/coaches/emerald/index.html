For the first time ever: a Gold Medal Scholar-Athlete Award for Emerald Youth.

Ken Chertow's Gold Medal Training Camps is awarding — for the first time ever — The Gold Medal Scholar-Athlete Award: a 50% scholarship toward Ken's six-day Olympic Gold Medal Training Camp, for wrestlers grinding to be champions on the mat and in the classroom. Emerald Youth Wrestling can send up to two — each on a 50% award.

Six days of Olympic-level coaching, elite practice partners, training and room & board at Spooky Nook · Hamilton, Ohio — July 18–23. A real drive from Knoxville — which makes a Gold Medal Training Camp more accessible to East Tennessee than it's ever been.

It's a Knoxville-wide program. Emerald Youth is one of the town's wrestling programs in it — and the goal is to fill a charter bus to Ohio with the best, hardest-working scholar-athletes from across Knoxville: the ones who put in the work and want it most.
